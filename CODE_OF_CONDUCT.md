# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately disruptive pull requests.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove comments,
commits, code, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and
may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviour they deem
inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer
privately. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
